# resources/

Product images are intentionally NOT shipped: the WH-1000XM3/XM4/MDR-1000X
renders are Sony marketing art (copyright + trademark). The app works without
them — it falls back to the themed `audio-headphones` icon. To show a product
image, drop your own `wh1000xm4.png` / `wh1000xm3.png` / `mdr1000x.png` here.
